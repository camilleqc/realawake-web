What Is a Dream Journal App?

A dream journal app is a place to write down your dreams as soon as you wake up, and to find them again later. Compared with paper, it makes your entries searchable and easy to review, so you can notice the people, places and feelings that keep coming back. If lucid dreaming is your goal, RealAwake works as a lucid dream journal too: choose the Lucid Dreams entry type and your lucid nights are easy to find later (here is how to keep a lucid dream journal). How to Choose a Dream Journal App

Whichever app you try, a few questions are worth asking:

  • Is it quick to write in? You'll use it half asleep, so fewer taps is better.
  • Who can read your entries? Check what's stored, where, and whether an AI reads it.
  • Are there ads? They can break the mood, and you're sharing something personal.
  • Can you find old dreams? Search, favorites and history matter more as entries pile up.
  • Can you tell lucid dreams apart? Useful if lucid dreaming is your goal.
  • Can you back it up? A journal is worth keeping safe.
